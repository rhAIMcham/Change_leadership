Question 1

What role does a leader play when guiding a team through change, and how can their decisions influence the outcome?

Saved!

A leader’s responsibility extends beyond making decisions or introducing new processes – they must communicate the reasons for change, establish priorities, support their team and reflect on their own behaviour towards others during uncertain times. Marie’s desire to protect quality and minimise risk led her to retain control and delay decisions. Effective leaders remain accountable for outcomes while adapting their approach to new situations, balancing business needs with the long-term development of staff and team members.

Question 2

How can leaders distinguish between technical problems and adaptive challenges when responding to change?

Saved!

Technical problems often have identifiable solutions, while adaptive challenges are more complex as they involve changing workplace culture, decision-making and other behaviours. At Canvas Creative, missed deadlines might appear to be a technical problem that could be resolved through better scheduling. However, the team’s dependence on Marie and reluctance to make independent decisions reveal deeper adaptive challenges.

Question 3

How can leaders identify the causes of resistance to change and respond in ways that encourage engagement?

Saved!

Resistance can manifest for many reasons, such as uncertainty and doubt, perceived loss of control, competing priorities or a lack of trust in leadership. Rather than assuming that people are unwilling to change, leaders should acknowledge concerns and consider how their own decisions or behaviours may be contributing to resistance. The team’s reluctance to take initiative is affected by Marie’s control over the studio, even when she asks staff to be more independent. By treating resistance as information that identifies what issues need attention, rather than an obstacle to overcome, leaders can build trust in change.
