Technical challenges vs. adaptive challenges

What factors should leaders consider when deciding whether a challenge requires a technical solution, an adaptive response, or a combination of both?

Saved!

Leaders must consider and analyse internal and external factors to enact meaningful organisational change, which can include fluctuating customer expectations and needs, market concerns and business positioning. Eliza Blank identified the technical limitations of a storefront and lack of knowledge as a barrier to purchasing plants and chose to adapt to a fully online experience for her customers while offering support for plant care to encourage consumers to purchase from her business.

Customer needs and underlying issues

How can leaders identify specific needs without alienating other potential customers?

Saved!

Effective leaders look beyond current trends or popular decision-making and take the time to identify what is driving customers to purchase from and support different businesses. While The Sill’s initial success may be credited to the current popularity of houseplants and other indoor garden features, they were also able to retain and expand their customer base by appealing to anyone, regardless of their personal experience caring for plants.

Change in a rapidly transforming world

How can leaders create the conditions for an organisation to adapt when existing approaches are already successful?

Saved!

When a business or organisation is successful, there may be less incentive to rapidly change or adapt to meet new expectations. It is important to identify what factors are responsible for this current success, if those factors are likely to change and how those factors may be affected in the future. By choosing to expand online, The Sill was able to meet new customer demands as they developed and encourage plant care at all stages of experience.
