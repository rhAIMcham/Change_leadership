Adaptive agility

How can an adaptive mindset help leaders view challenges as opportunities?

Saved!

Adaptive agility involves recognising changes in circumstances, making timely decisions with the information available and adjusting direction as new information emerges. Despite facing initial adversity, Meredith Farm successfully transitioned its business model to survive a long drought and other environmental concerns. By remaining open to different perspectives and treating setbacks as opportunities to learn, leaders can respond more effectively to change and produce better results.

Balancing innovation with consistency

What role does a leader play when guiding a team through change, and how can their decisions influence the wider organisation and industry?

Saved!

A leader’s role during change is to provide direction, communicate priorities and help others navigate uncertainty while remaining responsive to changing circumstances. The Camerons used their previous experience with farming and agriculture to guide their decisions when establishing Meredith Farm, while Sandy Cameron utilised her role as a veterinarian to provide a better quality of life for their animals, providing additional value for both the business and customers. Leaders do not have every answer, but they can create the conditions for success and the rapid development of new industry practices.

Building resilience while facing challenges

How can leaders build resilience while navigating change and other challenges?

Saved!

Effective change leadership does not mean achieving a perfect outcome or resolving every issue at once. Any transition may be difficult to navigate, requiring leaders to be resilient and reflective when guiding teams through change, especially if this change potentially impacts broader organisational function and security. As Meredith Farm prioritised sustainable and ethical farming practices, the Camerons were able to meaningfully respond to both criticism of their farming practices and their own environmental concerns without risking their businesses success. Recognising what you can control is crucial to finding constructive ways to navigate change.
