Identifying a problem or challenge to solve

How can leaders identify challenges or opportunities that require a different approach?

Saved!

Leaders can identify challenges and opportunities by observing changes in their environment, listening to others and questioning existing ways of working. Rather than immediately jumping to solutions, they should take time to understand the problem, consider different perspectives and identify what needs to change. Granger recognised that Sydney’s breakfast culture offered an opportunity to create a different dining experience by focusing on simple food and a social connection rather than formal dining.

The importance of leadership through change

How did Bill Granger and his business adapt to changing circumstances while maintaining a consistent vision, and why is this important for leaders during change?

Saved!

Effective leaders provide direction and maintain a clear sense of purpose while remaining flexible as circumstances evolve. Granger expanded Bills into international markets while staying consistent about what distinguished Bills from its competitors. This highlights the importance of flexibility in how change is implemented while maintaining clarity about the purpose, values and priorities that guide decisions.

Balancing innovation with consistency

How can leaders introduce change while maintaining the values and practices that are important to their team or organisation?

Saved!

Leaders must recognise which aspects of their work should evolve and which should stay consistent. They can encourage new ideas, respond to changing needs and improve existing practices while maintaining the values and standards that build trust and provide a shared sense of purpose. Granger demonstrated that innovation does not mean abandoning his business model, and identifying what made his business appealing to customers.
