Black Swan Minerals Announces Acquisition of OurSphere Consulting

Strengthening operational capability and positioning the business for long-term growth.

Black Swan Minerals is pleased to announce the successful acquisition with OurSphere Consulting, a respected environmental consultancy specialising in environmental assessment, sustainability and regulatory compliance.

The acquisition represents a significant milestone in the continued development of Black Swan Minerals and supports the organisation’s long-term business strategy. By uniting extensive mining industry experience with specialist environmental expertise, the new combined organisation aims to strengthen its operational capabilities, improve project delivery and support the growth of Australia’s resource sector.

As the Australian mining industry continues to evolve, this acquisition provides an opportunity to strengthen local industry capability, meet new environmental regulations and build a more resilient and competitive business.

Black Swan recognises the importance of maintaining business continuity throughout the transition. Existing projects, client commitments and operation requirements will remain a priority while these changes are progressively implemented.

We will continue to focus on delivering operational outcomes, maintaining industry standards and creating sustainable value for our stakeholders.
