Did You Know? Australia’s Endangered Species

Australia has the highest mammal extinction rate in the world.

From the numbat to the greater bilby to the orange-bellied parrot, many other unique Australian animals face ongoing threats from habitat destruction, introduced predators and environmental change.

Protecting biodiversity starts with understanding the environments we share and making informed decisions about how we manage and protect them.

Every habitat matters!

On the Ground: Supporting Mine Rehabilitation

Our team recently completed a site assessment as part of a mine rehabilitation project in Western Australia.

The work involved assessing vegetation recovery, monitoring soil conditions and identifying opportunities to improve habitat restoration.

Rehabilitation is a long-term process, and successful outcomes need ongoing monitoring and careful planning.

It’s great to see environmental improvement becoming more important for responsible resource management.
