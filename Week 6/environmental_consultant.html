A New Chapter for OurSphere and You: Creating Positive Change Through Collaboration

Bringing environmental sustainability and industry experience together to build a better future.

At OurSphere, we’ve always believed that protecting our planet requires more than just good intentions – it takes collaboration, inspiration and the preparation to challenge the way things have always been done.

That’s why we’re proud to announce our acquisition with Black Swan Minerals, marking an important new chapter for our organisation and the work we can achieve together.

Since our founding, OurSphere has worked alongside businesses and communities to support better environmental outcomes. From environmental assessments and rehabilitation planning to sustainability initiatives, our focus has always been on finding practical ways to protect the natural world and create lasting change.

Now, we’re bringing our expertise directly into a major Australian mining organisation. We see an opportunity to help influence decisions earlier and encourage a more integrated approach to responsible resource development.

We know that meaningful change doesn’t happen overnight. Bringing together two organisations with different backgrounds, experiences and ways of working will present challenges, but it also creates opportunities to learn from one another and rethink what is possible to achieve.

Our people and values that have shaped OurSphere remain an important part of this next chapter. The challenges facing our environment are complex, and no single organisation can address them alone. By combining our capabilities, we hope to contribute to a future where industry progress and environmental responsibility work more closely together.

Different experiences. A shared, sustainable future.

We’re excited about what comes next.
