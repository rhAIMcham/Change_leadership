Frequently asked questions

Welcome to Hoppers Insurance. We have proudly supported Australians at home and work for the last 30 years – let us support you next.

Read through the FAQs below and if you can’t find what you need, contact our friendly support staff for assistance.

How do I make a claim?

To make a claim, contact one of our agents. You will need your policy number and proof of identification.

How do I check the progress of my claim?

To check on the progress of your claim, contact your appointed agent via the email they provided in the first instance of your making a claim. If you can’t find or don’t have access to the email, submit a request via our support email and one of our staff will be in contact with you soon.

What documents do I need to provide for a claim?

The documents required vary depending on your circumstances. Your policy documents should contain these details, and are mailed to you each time the policy is renewed. Refer to your most recent policy to find specifics.

How long will my claim take?

Timeframes depend on the complexity of your claim. In your initial discussion with your agent, they will provide you with a timeline. Email or phone enquiries will be returned within 2 working days, and a timeline provided within that window.

How do I update my personal details?

Download a request for information change form here, and submit it via email. It will be implemented within 10 working days.

I can't find the answer to my question. What should I do?

Contact our support team via phone or email, or use the form below.
