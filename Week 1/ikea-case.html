• Retrain and retain

    IKEA is an example of organisational change where technology was introduced in tandem with a sincere investment in its existing workforce. The company introduced a chatbot to handle routine customer enquiries, reducing the need for call centre agents to dedicate time to answering repetitive questions. Rather than making these agents redundant, IKEA retrained them for roles that required more complex and personal customer support. These agents then provided services such as video consultations to support customers assemble products, offer interior design advice and assist with furniture configuration.

  • Resources where they are needed

    The change was beneficial for both the organisation and its workforce. The chatbot could reduce the demand on call centres by resolving straightforward enquires, while experienced staff focused on interactions where human judgement and personalised support provided additional value to customers. Retaining and retraining staff also meant IKEA maintained an experienced workforce that could potentially support the organisation should the chatbot technology need to be changed or replaced in the future. This approach demonstrates how leaders can consider the human impact of technological change and use retraining and redeployment to support staff while still achieving organisational objectives.
