Technical challenges and adaptive challenges

What aspects of this case study could be considered technical challenges, and what aspects could be considered adaptive challenges?

Saved!

Leaders need to consider what needs to change when resolving a challenge. Adobe’s move to Creative Cloud involved technical and commercial elements, such as a cloud functionality and changing software delivery. However, the change also required customers to adapt to a fundamentally different way of purchasing and using software, and Adobe needed to consider how the change affected different organisations and other users.

Adaptive leadership in practice

How does adaptive leadership principles assist leaders in managing a significant organisational change?

Saved!

Adaptive leadership encourages leaders to involve others in understanding the challenge and adjusting their approach as new information emerges. This would mean encouraging leaders to acknowledge different perspectives rather than relying solely on their own expertise or organisational authority. The internal changes to staff development also demonstrate how a leadership team can create greater ownership by giving team members and other leaders more agency in setting goals and adapting to new expectations.

Identifying future considerations

What considerations should leaders make when a change creates different benefits, challenges or impacts for different groups?

Saved!

Change rarely affects everyone in the same way. Leaders should consider the different needs, concerns and other potential impacts experience by staff, customers and other stakeholders. Adobe’s subscription model offers benefits like lower upfront costs, regular updates and greater connectivity, but also changed the financial aspect for customers who previously purchased software once and used it for many years.
