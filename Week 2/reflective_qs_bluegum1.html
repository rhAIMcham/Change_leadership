Identifying challenges

What were the challenges identified by the Bluegum leadership team?

Saved!

The leadership team identified ongoing communication issues between construction sites, including delays in sharing information, inconsistent processes and reliance on outdated communication methods and technology. While improving communication systems can address other inefficiencies, leaders also needed to understand how the changes would affect employees, their existing workflows and their ability to communicate in practice.

Adaptive challenges and technical problems

Were these challenges technical problems, adaptive challenges or a mixture of both?

Saved!

The Bluegum leadership team identified a technical problem – their communication processes were not sufficient in meeting the needs of contractors and clients and introduced new systems to better support project completion. However, this challenge was also an adaptive problem, as it required experienced site teams and contractors to adjust their ways of working and other responsibilities, which may eventually contribute to staff resistance.
