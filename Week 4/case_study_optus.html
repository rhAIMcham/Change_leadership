• Relocating

    In 2004, major telecommunications provider Optus approximately 6,500 employees from its North Sydney headquarters and eight other Sydney sites to a custom-built campus in Macquarie Park by 2007. This new facility offered modern office spaces, dedicated training and meeting rooms, green spaces, a gym, childcare and retail amenities. While the relocation promised greater opportunities for collaboration in improved facilities, it also introduced significant changes to employees’ daily routines.

  • Transport a challenge

    One of the most important challenges was the impact of the relocation on employees’ commutes. Although Macquarie Park was developing into a major business and technology precinct, delays to planned rail infrastructure meant public transport connections would not be ready when employees arrived at the new campus. With nearly 70% of staff relying on public transport, many faced the prospect of longer and more expensive journeys, unfamiliar routes, increased walking distances and greater exposure to weather. Additionally, the new campus had only 2,000 on-site parking spaces for the current 6,5000 Optus employees, with staffing expected to expand by another 500 individuals by 2008. Proactive management of the situation was necessary.


  • Practical solutions

    Recognising these concerns, Optus established an internal team to develop the Optus Sustainable Transport Strategy (OSTS). Through employee surveys, the team identified key commuting challenges and developed solutions focused on improving parking access, reducing travel needs and supporting alternatives to driving alone. Initiatives included incentives for car-pooling, a personalised journey planner and dedicated shuttle bus services connecting the campus with major transport hubs. On-site amenities, including childcare, a gym and retail facilities, were also intended to streamline the need for additional journeys before or after work.

  • Meeting demand

    The OSTS is widely regarded as a highly successful corporate travel plan in Australia. By recognising the stress, uncertainty and practical challenges employees faced when adjusting to a new workplace and unfamiliar commutes, Optus was able to respond to the needs and deliver solutions that exceeded expectations.
