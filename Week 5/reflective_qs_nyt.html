Leading through change

How can leaders balance the need to anticipate future change with the need to respond effectively to unexpected challenges and changing circumstances?

Saved!

Leaders need to remain aware of external influence, industry developments, technological advancements and changing customer expectations so they can identify potential risks and opportunities before they impact the organisation. By gradually recognising digital technology, changing reader expectations and new competitors would fundamentally change how people consumed news, The New York Times diversified their content and restructured their organisation. While its early digital initiatives were less successful, their willingness to adjust their approach and make timely decisions contributed to long-term success where their competitors struggled.

Managing competing priorities

When an organisation is experiencing significant change, how can leaders balance immediate operation demands with long-term goals?

Saved!

Change can create competing expectations, limited resources and uncertainty, making it difficult to satisfy every stakeholder while supporting the organisation. The New York Times demonstrates that successful transformation requires leaders to make difficult decisions and communicate a clear direction, while remaining aware of previous successes and the expectations of their existing customer base.

Building organisational adaptability

What makes an organisation capable of adapting and remaining successful in an environment of ongoing change?

Saved!

Leaders play an important role in creating an environment where employees feel supported to question existing practices and contribute ideas for improvement. Rather than relying solely on traditional news distribution, The New York Times leveraged its reputation against investing in new technologies and developing new capabilities while retaining a clear focus on the organisation’s core purpose. To be successful, this also requires organisations to monitor external pressures, recognise emerging risks and remain receptive to opportunities that may require significant changes to an organisation’s operational capabilities.
